  <h5 class="card-title">Efficient Feed-Forward 3D Gaussian Splatting via Hybrid Compaction</h5>
  <p class="card-text small text-muted mb-2">V-Lab, Yonsei University &middot; Jul. 2026 &ndash; Aug. 2026</p>
  <p class="card-text">A hybrid merge&ndash;prune compaction plug-in with per-scene optimization for feed-forward 3DGS. On tttLRM (CVPR 2026), it matched the fully optimized baseline on the DL3DV-140 benchmark at <strong>1/3 the peak VRAM</strong>. Extended to MVP (CVPR 2026), a view-dependent-opacity backbone, Local Pruning rendered <strong>4.3&times;/4.8&times; fewer primitives</strong> at 64/128 views while improving PSNR by <strong>0.84 dB</strong> at 64 views.</p>
